# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Tower Browser project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Give and accept technical
feedback in good faith. Do not harass, threaten, demean, dox, or publish another
person's private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns by opening a minimal issue requesting maintainer contact
without including private or sensitive details. Repository maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that
violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when
possible, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.
